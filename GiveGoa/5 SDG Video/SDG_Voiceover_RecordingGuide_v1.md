## Mark up the script (10 min)
1. Paste the full script into one document. Split it into the 4 parts from the Playbook: Problem 0:00–0:45, Role of data 0:45–2:00, Action 2:00–3:15, Close 3:15–3:45.
2. Read it aloud once with a timer. The total must be 3–4 minutes. If it is longer, ask Sahil which sentences to cut. Do not cut the source of the statistic.
3. Mark `/` for a short pause and `//` for a pause between parts. Underline the one number in each part.
4. Write how to say difficult words: uniRISE, RPwD, UDISE+, Shades of Purple.

## Record (40 min)
- Quiet room with soft things (bed, curtains). Fans and AC off. Phone on flight mode.
- Phone voice recorder app, phone 15–20 cm from your mouth, a little to the side (no breath noise).
- Record **one file for each part** (4 files). If you make a mistake, pause 2 seconds and repeat the full sentence. Rishabh cuts the mistakes.
- Record 5 seconds of silence at the start of file 1 (room tone for editing).
- Speak slower than normal, with a smile. Say each number clearly and the source after it ("…according to UDISE+ 2023–24…" — use the exact source in Sahil's script).
